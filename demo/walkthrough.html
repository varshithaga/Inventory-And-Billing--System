Turn this into a video

  1. Open this page full-screen in one browser tab.
  2. Start a screen recorder (OBS, Win + G, or Loom) on that tab.
  3. Press ↩ replay from scene 1 and let it auto-play through once (≈70 s).
  4. Stop the recording — that file is your demo video.

Recording the real app instead

  • Run seed_demo_data, start both servers.
  • Follow DEMO_SCRIPT.md in the repo — scene-by-scene clicks and narration.
